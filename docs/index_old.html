Welcome to Nivedita’s Blog

Hi, I’m Nivedita Bhadra — a Senior Data Scientist with a background in physics, computational modeling, large-scale data systems, and biomedical data science.

This blog is my personal learning space. I use it to document what I am learning, building, debugging, and understanding as I grow in data science, machine learning, statistical genetics, NLP, and modern AI tools.

I believe learning becomes deeper when we write it down clearly. Many of the articles here are written while I am learning something myself — testing code, fixing errors, building examples, and turning scattered notes into reusable tutorials.

My hope is that this blog helps people who want to follow a similar path: researchers moving into data science, beginners learning Python or R, scientists building computational skills, and professionals trying to make sense of modern machine learning workflows.

About Me

I work at the intersection of data science, machine learning, biomedical research, and large-scale computational analysis. My background is in physics and computational modeling, and over time I have moved toward applied machine learning, NLP, statistical genetics, and production-oriented data workflows.

On this blog, I share practical tutorials, hands-on notebooks, and lessons learned from real technical work.

Why I Write

I write because I am learning every day.

Some posts are polished tutorials. Some are technical notes. Some are lessons from debugging. But all of them are part of the same goal: to build a strong, honest, reusable knowledge base.

If you are also trying to grow in this field, I hope these notes make your journey a little easier.
