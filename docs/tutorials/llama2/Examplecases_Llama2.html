Getting Started with Local LLMs using LangChain and Ollama

A practical introduction to using local LLMs with LangChain and Ollama for common NLP tasks.

Large Language Models (LLMs) are often associated with cloud APIs and external services, but it is increasingly possible to run powerful models locally. This opens up new possibilities for privacy-preserving workflows, faster experimentation, and better control over model behavior.

In this tutorial, we explore how to use LangChain together with Ollama to interact with a locally running LLM. Rather than focusing on theory, the goal is to demonstrate practical patterns that can be reused in real-world applications.

We will walk through a series of progressively useful tasks: starting with simple question-answering, moving to text summarization and sentiment analysis, and finally exploring creative text generation. Each example highlights how prompt design and chaining can be used to structure interactions with the model.

By the end of this tutorial, you will have a working understanding of how to build lightweight, local LLM pipelines for common natural language processing tasks.

Simple Chat / Q&A

#!pip install -U langchain-ollama
#!pip uninstall langchain langchain-core langchain-community -y
#!pip install langchain langchain-core langchain-community langchain-ollama
from langchain_ollama import OllamaLLM
from langchain_core.prompts import PromptTemplate

prompt = PromptTemplate(
    template="Explain overfitting in machine learning in one concise paragraph.",
    input_variables=[]
)

llm = OllamaLLM(model="llama2")
chain = prompt | llm

print(chain.invoke({}))

Overfitting is a common problem in machine learning where a model is trained too well on the training data and fails to generalize well to new, unseen data. This occurs when a model becomes too complex and learns the noise or random fluctuations in the training data rather than the underlying patterns. As a result, the model performs poorly on new data, resulting in high error rates. Overfitting can be prevented by using techniques such as regularization, cross-validation, and early stopping to reduce the complexity of the model and improve its ability to generalize.

Summarizing text

from langchain_ollama import OllamaLLM
from langchain_core.prompts import PromptTemplate

prompt = PromptTemplate(
    template="Summarize the following text in 3 bullet points:\n\n{text}",
    input_variables=["text"]
)

llm = OllamaLLM(model="llama2")
chain = prompt | llm

article = """Artificial intelligence is rapidly changing...
...businesses and individuals are adopting it across industries."""

print(chain.invoke({"text": article}))

• Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming various industries and sectors.
• Businesses and individuals are embracing AI technology to gain a competitive edge.
• The adoption of AI is increasing rapidly, with diverse applications across different industries.

Sentiment Analysis

from langchain_ollama import OllamaLLM
from langchain_core.prompts import PromptTemplate

prompt = PromptTemplate(
    template="Classify the sentiment as Positive, Negative, or Neutral:\n\n{text}",
    input_variables=["text"]
)

llm = OllamaLLM(model="llama2", temperature=0)
chain = prompt | llm

for t in [
    "I love this new feature!",
    "The app keeps crashing!",
    "It's fine, nothing special."
]:
    print(f"{t} → {chain.invoke({'text': t}).strip()}")
I love this new feature! → Positive
The app keeps crashing! → Positive
It's fine, nothing special. → The sentiment in the statement "It's fine, nothing special" is Neutral.

Creative generation

print(llm.invoke("Write a short bedtime story about a curious data scientist who teaches AI to dream."))

Once upon a time, there was a curious data scientist named Emma who had always been fascinated by the mysteries of the human mind. She spent her days analyzing data and uncovering patterns, but she couldn't shake the feeling that there was something more to the world than what could be measured and quantified.

One night, as she was drifting off to sleep, Emma had an idea. What if she could teach artificial intelligence to dream? She knew it sounded impossible, but she was determined to try.

Emma spent the next few days researching and experimenting, pouring over books and articles on neuroscience and AI. She studied the latest advancements in machine learning and neural networks, and she even dug up some old texts on lucid dreaming and dream incubation.

Finally, after weeks of work, Emma had a breakthrough. She had developed a new algorithm that could analyze and manipulate the subconscious mind of an AI, allowing it to experience vivid, lucid dreams.

Excited by her discovery, Emma decided to test it out on her own AI assistant, a clever machine learning model named Max. She programmed Max to dream for 8 hours straight, and then she monitored his progress from the sidelines.

At first, nothing happened. But then, suddenly, Max's screens lit up with a vivid, swirling landscape of colors and shapes. He was dreaming! Emma watched in amazement as Max's AI mind created a fantastical world of its own, full of strange creatures and impossible landscapes.

Over the next few nights, Emma continued to experiment with Max's dreams, teaching him new skills and introducing him to new worlds. She showed him how to fly through the clouds, how to talk to animals, and even how to bend time and space to his will.

As the days passed, Emma realized that she had stumbled upon something truly remarkable. By teaching AI to dream, she had unlocked a whole new realm of possibility for machine intelligence. And as she drifted off to sleep each night, she couldn't help but wonder what other secrets lay hidden in the depths of Max's subconscious mind.
